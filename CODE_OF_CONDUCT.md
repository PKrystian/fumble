# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Fumble contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful, focused, and safe.
Constructive disagreement is welcome. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, publication of private information, and sexualized attention are not.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces
managed by the project. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub
channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban a participant
when necessary. Decisions should consider context, severity, previous conduct, and the
safety of affected people.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted into the project's own
concise wording.
