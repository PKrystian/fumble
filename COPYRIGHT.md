## Reporting material

Good-faith rights-holder requests can be submitted through:

- the contact channels at https://github.com/PKrystian
- the content-report template at https://github.com/PKrystian/Fumble/issues/new/choose

Include the exact public URL or repository path, identification of the protected work, the
basis on which you are authorized to act, the requested correction or removal, and a safe
method for follow-up.

Do not publish confidential information, identity documents, signatures, or unnecessary
personal data in a public issue. A substantiated request may result in the affected data
source being disabled or removed while the report is reviewed.
