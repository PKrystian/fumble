## Summary

[[Mira Voss]] has noticed ships unloading at Silverhaven's wharves after dark, off the
books. She keeps a private ledger of them - but she won't share it with strangers.

## Objectives

- [ ] Earn Mira's trust around the harbor.
- [ ] Learn who is smuggling cargo past the [[Silverhaven Harbor Guild]].

## Leads

Ask the dockworkers. Watch the far wharf at night. Notice which manifests don't add up.

:::locked
There's a name in the ledger the party will recognize.
:::

:::secret
DM note: the ledger points at the Black Citadel's smuggling route through [[The Sunken
Temple]]. This is the hook into the main plot.
:::
