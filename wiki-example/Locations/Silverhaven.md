**Silverhaven** is a bustling port city on the Argent Coast, famous for its silver trade
and towering lighthouse. Its harbor is run by [[Mira Voss]], a steady hand who's seen
every ship the tide has brought in.

## History

Founded centuries ago by sailors fleeing a sunken homeland, Silverhaven grew rich on the
silver veins in the nearby cliffs. The [[Silverhaven Harbor Guild]] has watched over its
docks ever since.

:::locked
The city's true founders were a cabal of liches who still slumber beneath the harbor.
:::
