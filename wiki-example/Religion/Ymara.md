**Ymara, the Tidemother** is the sea given a face - generous with calm waters, terrible
when slighted. Every ship leaving [[Silverhaven]] pours a cup over the rail for her.

## Worship

Her shrines stand at harbor mouths, hung with lanterns and knotted rope. Offerings are
simple: fresh water, a coin, a promise kept.

## Tenets

- Guide those who are lost back to shore.
- Take nothing from the sea you will not return.

:::locked
The oldest hymns name a second face of Ymara, worshipped beneath the waves.
:::

:::secret
