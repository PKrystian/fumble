The **Silverhaven Harbor Guild** licenses every pilot, weighs every cargo, and collects
the harbor tariff that keeps the lighthouse lit. If a ship docks at [[Silverhaven]], the
Guild has a record of it.

## Structure

Apprentices work the wharves; licensed pilots guide ships past the reef; the harbormaster
speaks for them all.

## Standing

Broadly trusted, occasionally resented for its tariffs — but no one questions that the
Guild keeps the harbor safe.

:::locked
The Guild's oldest ledgers hint at a debt owed to something beneath the waves.
:::

:::secret
DM note: two senior pilots are on the Black Citadel's payroll and forge cargo manifests.
:::
