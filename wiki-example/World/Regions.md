This map uses the **Obsidian leaflet plugin** syntax directly. Coordinates are image
pixels (leaflet's bottom-left origin); markers typed `dm` stay hidden from players.

```leaflet
image: [[world.svg]]
marker: default, 310, 224, [[Silverhaven]]
marker: default, 210, 512, [[The Sunken Temple]]
marker: dm, 460, 640, [[Secret Plot]]
```
