The **Tideglass Lantern** is carved from a single pane of blue-green tideglass. Its flame
holds steady through gale and downpour, and sailors swear it points the way home.

## Powers

While lit, the lantern sheds steady light no wind can extinguish, and its glow turns a
deeper blue as danger nears the water.

## History

Pulled from the flooded halls of [[The Sunken Temple]], it now hangs at the [[Silverhaven]]
harbor gate. The faithful call it a gift of [[Ymara]].

:::locked
Held underwater, the lantern is said to reveal a door that isn't there in daylight.
:::

:::secret
DM note: the lantern is a key to the Temple's lower vault. The Black Citadel wants it back.
:::
