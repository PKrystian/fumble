The **Harbor Drake** is a sleek, salt-scarred cousin of true dragons, no bigger than a
rowboat. It trails fishing craft for easy meals and sulks in the cliff caves outside
[[Silverhaven]].

## Behavior

Territorial but rarely deadly - it prefers theft to a fight. A drake that has tasted
cargo, though, grows bold enough to raid the wharves.

## Sightings & Lore

Dockworkers blame every missing crate on "the drake." Some of the time, they're right.

:::locked
Fishers say a much larger drake nests deeper in the caves. No one has come back to confirm.
:::

:::secret
DM note: use a wyvern stat block for the larger cave-nesting drake. It guards a wrecked
smuggler's hold.
